# Director brief

**Product.** Agent Hangar: one home for every AI agent a company builds. Agents are built from the AI tool people
already use (over MCP), tested, run in their own container, governed (approvals, budgets, audit) and used from any
platform. Since v0.16, an agent can be hired as a **Digital employee**: a job, a manager and an authority level that
the platform enforces before every action.

**Audience.** Heads of platform, IT and engineering at companies with many AI pilots and no place to run them.
Technical, skeptical of hype.

**What they must remember.** "My agents get one home, and the platform — not the prompt — decides what they may do."

**Tone.** Confident, precise, calm. Short sentences. No hype words. Let the real screens prove it.

**Reference (style only, never content).** Linear's product launch videos: dark, quiet surfaces; tight crops of the
real interface with slow camera moves; one short line of kinetic type per idea; hard cuts on the beat; almost no
effects. We take the restraint and the rhythm, not their layout, words or music.

**Music.** 120 BPM, synthesized in code: a soft kick, a short bass motif in A minor, light hats from bar 3; it opens up
at the "Digital employee" turn and resolves on the logo.

**Formats.** 16:9, 60 s (site, README, Product Hunt). 9:16, 30 s (LinkedIn, Reels): the hook, the Digital employee
turn and the close.

**Call to action.** "Open source. One command to install." + `github.com/valteresj2/agent-hangar`.
